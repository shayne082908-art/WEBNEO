# Current State

Writable: **Yes**

- offline Idea Forge

- genre and mood controls
- lock individual prompt ingredients and reroll everything else
- saved sparks per book
- copied prompts
- pinned prompt panel above the manuscript

## Known limitations
- The local Forge is intentionally manuscript-agnostic. Context-aware ideation belongs to the optional AI layer later.
- Random combinations can occasionally be tonally odd; prompt-pool tuning will follow playtest feedback.
- Browser storage may be cleared by the user/browser, so explicit backups still matter.
